# Design Guidelines — ai4UCheE

## Visual Language & Institutional Ivory Surface (`ai4UMasterplan` Sovereign Design System)
`ai4UCheE` implements a single, clean **Institutional Ivory (`#FBF6EC` / `data-theme="paper"`)** visual identity (Dark Navy `#071A33` mode removed for simplicity):

1. **Institutional Ivory (`#FBF6EC` / `data-theme="paper"`)**:
   - **Canvas & Surfaces**: Primary Institutional Ivory (`--ivory: #fbf6ec`), Secondary Paper panels (`--paper: #f5f0e0`), Elevated cards (`#ffffff`).
   - **Typography & Ink**: Sovereign Deep Navy (`--navy-deep: #071a33`) primary text, Muted Slate (`--muted-foreground: #3b455d`, WCAG 2.2 AA $\ge 4.5:1$ contrast).
   - **Masthead & Brand**: Sovereign Deep Navy header (`#071a33`) with the canonical `ai4U` logo (`/ai4u-logo.png`, Maize drop-shadow glow), `Cormorant Garamond` display title, and Maize Gold (`--maize: #e8c547`) eyebrow & hairline border.
   - **Accents**: Maize Gold (`--maize: #e8c547`, `--maize-soft: #f7dca6`) for active workbench tabs, Point-and-Ask selection halos, and citation badges; Navy (`--navy: #0f1b3d`) for primary traces.

## Typography
- **Display & Section Headings**: `Cormorant Garamond`, serif (weights 500, 600, 700).
- **UI & Narrative Body**: `Karla`, sans-serif (weights 400, 500, 600, 700).
- **Calculations, Units & Citations**: `JetBrains Mono` (`tabular-nums`) and `KaTeX`.

## Interactive Ergonomics
- **Point-and-Ask (`useAgentSelectable`)**: Clicking any calculation result, table cell, or diagram region attaches an `agent-select:<kind>:<id>` context pill to the chat composer with a `--maize` (`#e8c547`) selection outline.
- **Scrubbable Numeric Inputs**: Engineering parameters (flow rate, temperature, pipe diameter) support drag-to-scrub manipulation with immediate live re-calculation.
- **Visual Grounding**: Calculations link directly to side-by-side PDF crops showing the handbook source equation and table row.

## Sovereign Three-File CSS & Framer "Luxurious Three" Motion Architecture
- **Three-file CSS split, each `< 200` LOC**: `src/styles/theme-tokens.css` (palette tokens, typography, `.app-shell` with `grid-template-rows: auto auto auto 1fr`, and `@import './workbench-components.css'` + `@import './framer-motion-surfaces.css'`), `src/styles/workbench-components.css` (tactile `.scrubbable-*`, `.motion-pill`, `.mission-*`, `.learn-callout`, `.chat-drawer`, `.chip-tray`, `.composer-chip`, `.genui-*`, `.wb-verdict`, `.wb-crop-placeholder`, `.modal-backdrop`, `.katex-block`), and `src/styles/framer-motion-surfaces.css` (Framer MCP `bPWSkk20pwfxzXmKGtRh` radial spotlights, `.framer-pill-glow`, `.swarm-pulse-dot` status beacons, SVG drag handle drop-shadow halos, `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`, and `@media print`).
- **Framer "Luxurious Three" Motion System (`src/lib/framer-motion-tokens.ts` & `src/components/ui/framer-motion-primitives.tsx`)**:
  - **Appear / Entrance (`FramerEntranceStage`)**: Enter-only `opacity: 0 → 1, y: 18 → 0` with cubic-bezier `[0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]` (`0.65s`, `60ms` stagger via `getFramerStaggerTransition`) and zero exit-hold latency so Vitest synchronous unmount checks pass in `0ms`.
  - **Shared-Layout Pill Spring (`MotionPillButton`, `FRAMER_LAYOUT_SPRING`)**: `{ type: 'spring', stiffness: 500, damping: 60, mass: 1 }` across header tabs, persona switcher, `📐 Charts & Page Verifier`, `<DiagramModeBar />` (`WB2`, `WB3`, `WB4`, `WB6`), `WB1` 10-chart presets, and `WB5` cluster & 25-section pills.
  - **Swarm Pulse Telemetry (`SwarmPulseDot`, `FRAMER_SWARM_PULSE_TRANSITION`)**: `7px` breathing status orb (`opacity: 0.35 → 1, scale: 1 → 1.25`, `1.4s` mirror loop) inside `dof-badge`, Co-Pilot badges, `<MissionChecklistBanner />`, `WB5` status banner, and `calc-sheet-safety-badge`.
  - **SVG Scrub Guard**: Never apply `scale()` or `translateY()` hover transforms to `.wb-crop-stage` or `<svg>` elements so `getBoundingClientRect()` inside `clientPointToViewBox` remains pixel-exact.
- **Nested-grid discipline**: `.shell-workspace` (outer AppShell `<main>`, `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1.78fr) minmax(320px, 370px)`) vs. `.wb-split` (inner workbench splits, `1.42fr 1fr`). Both are equal-specificity single-class selectors, so `.shell-workspace` is deliberately pinned AFTER `.wb-split` in source order (cascade pin) to win on `<main class="shell-workspace wb-split">`; both collapse to `1fr` below 1080 px.
- **Minimalist 3-Zone AI-Native Studio Architecture ("1 Bar · 1 Canvas · 1 Agent")**:
  - **Zone 1 (Compact Problem-First Top Chrome)**: Ultra-compact `.shell-header` (`48px`), single-line `.mission-bar` focusing on `📚 Browse All 24 Problems (🌱 L1 · 🔧 L2 · 🕵️ L3 · 🚨 L4)` with a floating popover `<ProblemLibraryBrowser />` (`position: fixed; z-index: 80`), collapsible `.studio-drawer-collapsed` quick-missions & student scorecard drawer (keeping DOM nodes mounted for instant accessibility and test invariants while taking `0px` visual height when closed), and a compact 30px single-line subject pill switcher (`nav.wb-toolbar`).
  - **Zone 2 (Visual Canvas + 34px Single-Line Dials First)**: Every workbench (`WB2`, `WB3`, `WB4`, `WB6`, `WB5`) places its primary interactive SVG schematic/chart and single-line `34px` Framer-style property-row `<ScrubbableNumberInput />` dials (`grid-template-columns: minmax(135px, 1.05fr) minmax(150px, 1.35fr)`) at the top of the canvas, with `<WhiteBoxEquationCard />` derivations, `<DiagramModeBar />` galleries, and compact `<LearnModeCallout />` progressive-disclosure drawers cleanly below. Nested `.wb-panel .wb-panel` surfaces are flattened with zero radial-gradient or gold-border repetition.
  - **Zone 3 (Minimalist AI Co-Pilot Inspector — `<AgentChatDrawer />`)**: Clean 1-line header (`🤖 AI Co-Pilot`), prominent central message & inline `genui-stream` proof area (`flex: 1`), Point-and-Ask context chip tray (`composer-chip-tray`), single-row horizontal pill rails (`overflow-x: auto; white-space: nowrap`) for `copilot-quick-actions` and `copilot-starter-prompts`, and a bottom natural-language/What-If composer.
- **`<KaTeXBlock />`**: safe `katex.renderToString` wrapper (`throwOnError: false`) for display & inline equations — DIPPR Eq. 101/106 (WB2), the Darcy–Fanning guardrail (WB3), Fenske `N_min` (WB4), and every inline `equation-proof-card` GenUI card. Never render equations as ASCII monospace.
- **`<GuidedMissionBar />`**: 5 one-click student missions under the nav (`Catch the 4× Friction Trap`, `Boil Water at 100 °C`, `Check Pump Cavitation`, `Stage a Binary Distillation Column`, `Stop Reactor Runaway & Pore Diffusion`) while the main navigation bar holds the 5 pure Chemical Engineering subjects (`WB2`, `WB3`, `WB4`, `WB6`, `WB5`) and the header utility bar exposes the cross-subject `📐 Charts & Page Verifier` tool (`WB1`). A mission click switches to 🎓 Learn Mode, selects the target workbench, attaches the mission's Point-and-Ask chip, and instantly emits an `equation-proof-card` into the Co-Pilot drawer's GenUI stream (collapsible via the toggle button).
- **`<LearnModeCallout />`**: plain-English student intuition card (title / plain-English explanation / ⚠️ common trap / Perry anchor) rendered only when `personaMode === 'learn'` so Plant Mode keeps its compact EPC workspace.
